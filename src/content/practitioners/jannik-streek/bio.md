Jannik Streek is a software engineer and co-founder of B310 Digital, a software company based in Kiel, Germany, that helps organizations with open-source initiatives such as GitLab. His work focuses primarily on open source and collaborative software, such as TeamMapper, a mind-mapping tool.
Since 2025, he has been using agentic coding tools extensively in his day-to-day software development work. He has led workshops at several companies, helping engineering teams adopt agentic coding workflows. His current focus is on translating stakeholder requirements into clear, actionable instructions for AI coding agents and closing the agentic loop through automated testing and feedback using Playwright and other tools. He is also a speaker on agentic coding and has served as an instructor in service-oriented architecture at a university of applied sciences. He holds bachelor's and master's degrees in IT-Systems Engineering from the Hasso Plattner Institute in Potsdam.
