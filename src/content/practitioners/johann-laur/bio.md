Johann is a software developer and Lead of Technical Operations at Tudock in Hamburg. His work is
focused on ERP systems and the interfaces that connect them to e-commerce, where order, stock and
product data have to move between systems that were never designed for each other. At h&w he
delivers Agentic Product Engineering as an enablement training, the two-day workshop where
engineers learn to work with coding agents.
