Romy Koitzsch-Wagener works freelance as an agile and AI consultant and comes to it from project management. She has advised teams on how they organise their work and where AI belongs in it. At hackers&wizards she runs operating support: she carries a training from preparation through to the certificates, coordinates course partnerships, and picks up the exceptions the delivery process cannot settle on its own. She is building her own training work in product-oriented formats, where a room asks less about code than about how a team decides, plans and ships.
