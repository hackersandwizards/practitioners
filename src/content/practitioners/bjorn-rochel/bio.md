Björn is an independent consultant for agentic engineering, working with small companies and large
corporations on rolling it out across their engineering organizations. Earlier he was Senior Manager
Engineering at Wellfound and Director Architecture at XING, where he introduced GraphQL and led the
API migrations that followed. That is where he learned change management and how to get a large
initiative over the line, which is most of the work. At h&w he specializes in Agentic Factory
Engineering, though he also runs the full arc from zero to factory. His March 2026 talk at Agentic
Conf Hamburg argued that a better harness beats a bigger model, which was a lite software factory
before the term caught on.
