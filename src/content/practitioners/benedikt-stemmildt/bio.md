Benedikt Stemmildt is Founder & Co-CEO of hackers&wizards in Hamburg, which runs AI training and
mentoring in agentic engineering for engineering and product teams, so that the speed they gain
shows up for their customers. He has been a technologist since he was a kid, and for twenty years
one thing has driven him: developers in flow, building great products. He co-wrote Building the
Agentic Software Factory with Alex Merced (Packt), hosts the Rethink Engineering podcast, is program
chair of code.talks and curates CASE Berlin. Before that he worked as an engineer, architect and CIO
at OTTO, Breuninger and BLUME2000.
