Matthias specializes in software factories and helps companies build them: through coaching or
hands-on together with the client's team. He has been developing software for more than 20 years,
15 of them as a freelancer, and in that time got to know many teams, their workflows and their
domains. He carries both over into agentic systems. To him, agents are new teammates: they have to
know a team's workflows and its domain just like everyone else. At hackers&wizards he supports
teams through mentoring while they build their software factory and develop it further.
