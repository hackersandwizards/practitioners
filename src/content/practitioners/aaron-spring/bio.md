Aaron did a PhD in climate science, then worked as an ML and AI engineer at XING. He now freelances
in Hamburg on machine learning and geospatial cloud data, and his niche is climate data as a service
at petabyte scale. He is a maintainer of climpred, an open source package for verifying weather and
climate forecasts. At h&w he delivers Agentic Product Engineering, the two-day workshop where
engineers learn to work with coding agents.
