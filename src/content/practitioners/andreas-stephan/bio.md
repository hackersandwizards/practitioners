Andreas Stephan is an AI-native product manager from Hamburg, a former developer himself, and has
been building digital products for more than 20 years, mainly in e-commerce and online media. As
interim product lead he works embedded in the team and explores how AI-native ways of working scale
from the individual to whole teams and organizations. He is co-author of hycle, an operating model
for product and tech organizations.
