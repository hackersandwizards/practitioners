Verena Traub joins hackers&wizards in January 2027 to take over Developer Relations. She will look
after the conferences, meetups and events where engineers meet the company, write about what she
hears there, lead the trainer community, and prepare and follow up client trainings.

She is a cloud native engineer. After more than a decade in tech recruiting and HR, she moved into
engineering and discovered her passion for building and operating modern cloud infrastructure. She
has helped teams run and scale their infrastructure with Kubernetes and GitOps, bridging the gap
between technical implementation, collaboration, and organizational realities, and she is exploring
where agentic workflows work best.

She speaks and moderates at cloud native and DevOps conferences across Europe, including KubeCon
Europe, DevOpsDays and code.talks. In 2023 she co-founded [reher*sal](https://rehersal.io/), an
initiative dedicated to empowering FLINTA* voices on tech stages. She is a Women Techmakers
Ambassador and a CDF Ambassador.
