Alexander is CTO at YogaEasy in Hamburg, where he owns the video streaming platform with 800,000
users end to end, the Rails code as much as the product, marketing, CRM and accounting it serves.
He reads a technical decision from the management side first, what it costs and who has to live
with it, and he ships production code with coding agents every day. The management half comes from
thirteen years at XING, the last ten as Director of Engineering, where his platform teams enabled
more than a hundred developers in twenty-odd domain teams to ship unified apps to 13 million users.
At h&w he delivers two-day trainings for developer and QA teams, runs train-the-trainer cohorts,
and mentors engineering leaders. His rooms are often mixed, architects, product owners and a
division head at one table, so he prepares more material than a day needs and gives each of them
an answer to the question they came with. In his free time he works on music with agents at hand,
from electronic instruments to restoring historic originals, and plays live with his band.
