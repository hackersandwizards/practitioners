Kamil Kubica is an AI solution architect and co-owner of nice solutions GmbH, which builds AI
software for companies, from the first prototype to a solution that runs in daily operation. He has
developed software for more than 20 years, over ten of them self-employed as a software and solution
architect, for e-commerce, industry, aviation, healthcare and media. He was responsible for the
architecture and implementation of [Wegweiser Gesundheit](https://www.wegweisergesundheit.de/de), a
health portal whose AI assistant answers employees' questions on health and occupational safety in
eight languages. With hackers&wizards he runs two-day workshops in which product and development
teams learn to build software together with AI agents. In one client codebase he opened 35 years of
legacy code to coding agents through a controlled MCP interface, without leaving the existing
security rules. With teams that want to go further he builds an AI software factory: a pipeline of
agents that takes work from the requirement to the code, with automated quality gates along the way.
