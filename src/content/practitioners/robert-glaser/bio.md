Robert Glaser is Head of Applied AI at Exxeta. He works with organizations on agentic engineering and the software factory as an operating model for product teams. His focus is on reliable delegation to agents, product quality, and turning experience from daily work into shared capabilities. He writes [The Elastic Loop](https://elastic-loop.robert-glaser.de/), an agent-ready book about human–agent collaboration. Previously, he spent 16 years at INNOQ, where he built and led the Data & AI practice.
