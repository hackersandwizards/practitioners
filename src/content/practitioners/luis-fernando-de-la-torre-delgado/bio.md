Luis Fernando De La Torre Delgado is a cloud and AI solutions architect in Hamburg with eighteen years in software engineering. FinTech was where he first built on AWS; the circular economy years were where he learned what it takes to keep a system scaling once real load arrives. Today he builds agentic AI systems, on AWS or on European infrastructure when the data is not allowed to leave the continent. For a railway client he delivered a construction plan review system alone in five weeks. It found every gap that had been planted in the test set and invented none. With h&w he takes on that kind of build and the two-day workshop that goes with it, where teams learn what an agent is asked to do, what it knows while doing it, how its work is verified and what it is allowed to touch.
