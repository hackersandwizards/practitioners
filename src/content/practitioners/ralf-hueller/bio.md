*"In the current AI cycle, we are always the experts for the next six months. So: carpe diem!"*

Ralf is an AI Solution Architect and builds AI systems that act on their own, not just answer. His
current project HitsBrain, an agent-based assistant system running in a sandbox, has shipped as a
pilot, each customer on a dedicated VPS with exclusive access. Behind that are more than 20 years
as a full-stack developer and solution architect, plus experience as a project lead in enterprises
and SMEs in the energy sector; his software still runs in production at an energy utility,
connected to SAP IS-U.

Ralf is a member of [Havel Valley](https://havelvalley.com/), a volunteer-run initiative that
wants to make Brandenburg an der Havel a place for sustainable business and work just outside
Berlin. As an independent supporter he brings his AI know-how to meetups and coworking and helps
founders and freelancers from the region bring agents into their daily work.
