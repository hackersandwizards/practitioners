Markus Andrezak founded überproduct, a product and strategy boutique in Berlin and Potsdam that
offers the cohort seminar "AI-Augmented Product Management". He brings more than 15 years of
experience with internet products, employed or as a contractor at eBay Classifieds Group, eBay, AOL,
Scout-Gruppe, MyHammer, Gruner & Jahr and mobile.de. He argues for data-driven rather than
taste-driven product decisions and hosts the podcast "Stories Connecting Dots".
