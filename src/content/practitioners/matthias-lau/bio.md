Matthias Lau is a developer and founder of Heureka Labs, a Hamburg technology studio. He builds AI
systems, robots and the [AI Router](https://airouter.io) for model routing, and researches
knowledge graphs as memory for agents. He speaks at conferences such as code.talks and Future
Coding Day. His own agentic software factory runs on three harnesses, Claude Code, Codex and Pi,
and routes each task to whichever model fits best.
